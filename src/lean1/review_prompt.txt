## Questions you must answer

1. Statement fidelity. Does the conjunction of the verified theorems state the answer to the exact question on the website, with the same objects, parameter ranges, quantifiers, conventions, and scope? A theorem about a special case, a subclass, a restricted parameter range, a modified definition, a weaker notion, or a different convention does not resolve the problem, unless the website's statement itself has that scope.
2. Definitions. Are the formal definitions faithful to the standard mathematical meaning used by the problem and its sources? Look in particular for definitions that make the statement vacuous or trivial: empty domains, a quantity defined as a constant, a supremum over an empty or degenerate set, missing normalization, truncated or finite-dimensional surrogates the problem does not specify, or a "solution" encoded as a hypothesis.
3. Hypotheses. Does any theorem carry an assumption that the problem does not grant, assume an open conjecture, or assume the conclusion? Are all hypotheses satisfiable in the intended setting?
4. Direction of the answer. For a yes/no question, does the result prove the complete positive claim or give a genuine counterexample within the stated scope? A counterexample must satisfy every requirement of the problem.
5. Prior work. Is the result merely a case already listed under "progress", or otherwise known? A known partial result is not a resolution.
6. Informal steps. Does result.md rely on mathematical claims that are not covered by the verified theorems, such as a reduction from the website's formulation to the formal statement? If so, check each such step rigorously; one unproved or incorrect step means rejection.
7. Reproduction. Recompile the listed files yourself and print the axioms of each listed theorem, for example with `#print axioms` in a scratch file under .lean1/review/. Confirm that no sorry, admit, custom axiom, or native_decide is used.

Default to rejection when you are uncertain. Accept only when you would sign a referee report stating that the problem, as posed on the website, is resolved by these verified theorems together with correct informal arguments.
